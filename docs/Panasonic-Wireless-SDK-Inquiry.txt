Dear LUMIX SDK team,

I am developing Cam Link, an independent Android application for event photography. The intended workflow is to receive original JPEGs immediately after capture over Wi-Fi, retain a local copy, and upload them to Google Drive. Target cameras are the DC-S1RM2 and DC-S1M2. The application should connect directly, without requiring LUMIX Lab or LUMIX Tether to run alongside it.

Could you advise whether developer access is available to the current encrypted wireless tethering protocol, an Android-compatible SDK, or a supported platform-independent API?

The public SDK page lists Windows libraries with USB and selected Ethernet support. I have not found documentation for implementing the newer wireless tethering protocol on Android. I understand that the public beta SDK does not include technical support; this is an access/partnership enquiry, and a referral to the appropriate team would be appreciated.

Specifically, I would like to confirm:

1. Support for DC-S1RM2 firmware 1.60 and DC-S1M2, including minimum firmware requirements.
2. Discovery, user-approved pairing, authentication, certificate handling, encrypted sessions, keep-alive and reconnection requirements.
3. New-photo notifications and original JPEG retrieval during burst shooting, while retaining files on the camera card and avoiding changes to shooting settings.
4. Support for both direct camera Wi-Fi and a shared wireless access point.
5. Developer registration, NDA, fees, commercial distribution terms, and any Android SDK redistribution requirements.

An attached summary describes read-only diagnostics on a DC-S1RM2 running firmware 1.60. The camera is reachable, but our standard PTP/IP prototype has not established a tethering session or received any JPEGs. We are seeking the supported integration path, not a way to bypass authentication.

Thank you for your guidance or referral.

Best regards,
Cam Link developer
